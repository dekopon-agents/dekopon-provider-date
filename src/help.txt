Usage: date [-u | --utc | --timezone IANA] [--days SIGNED_DAYS] [+FORMAT]
       date --help

Print the fresh broker clock (UTC RFC3339 to seconds by default).
--timezone=IANA and --days=SIGNED_DAYS are also accepted.
Quote +FORMAT as one shell argument when it contains spaces.
Supported conversions: %F %Y %m %d %H %M %S %z %Z %s %%
Formats: at most 256 printable ASCII bytes; no flags, widths, or modifiers.
Days: -36600..36600 local calendar days, preserving wall time, not 86400s.
Nonzero offsets landing in a timezone gap or fold fail; zero keeps the instant.
Limits: 8 arguments, 1024 total argument bytes, 64 timezone bytes, 2048 output bytes.
No TZ environment, date parsing, clock setting, or GNU/BSD compatibility.
Help and command proposals never read the clock; clock.now requires broker authorization.
